About us

The leading manufacturer of quality furniture and shop fittings in Zimbabwe.

Calundike Exports is a member of the Nyaradzo Group, specialising in the manufacturing of furniture, fittings and caskets. We are based in Ruwa, 25 km east of Harare, with another factory in Gweru.

We pride ourselves in functional, quality, aesthetically pleasing furniture - artistically designed and customised to your specifications, delivering exceptional value for your home and office.
